The idea

An intimate taberna, built for sharing.

Maré Social takes its name from the Portuguese word for tide — a rhythm that never sits still. Cold and hot tapas move across the table in waves, followed by mains, sushi, and dessert.

The menu is guided by Henrique Sá Pessoa, translating refined technique into josper-grilled seafood, cured meats, and a Nikkei sushi counter. Iberian and Japanese references share one table.

Where it comes from

Two coastlines, one table.

The menu follows a path from Portuguese cooking to the Nikkei counter.

Lisbon

Portuguese taberna cooking

Grilled meats and seafood, croquettes, and fish soup — rustic food with refined technique.

Iberia & Mediterranean

Sharing plates

Burrata, salads, batatas bravas, and paella designed to move around the table.

Nikkei

Japanese-Peruvian influence

Nigiri, sashimi, tiraditos, and crudos bring Japanese technique together with South American citrus and chili.
